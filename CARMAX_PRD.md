# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# CARMAX Premium Automotive Website

## Vision

Build a premium, modern, animated website for CARMAX, a luxury
multi-brand automotive workshop in Mangalore.

## Goals

-   Showcase premium services
-   Generate bookings and WhatsApp enquiries
-   Display modifications, accessories, and completed projects
-   Improve SEO and brand perception

## Target Audience

-   Luxury car owners
-   SUV owners
-   Car enthusiasts
-   Fleet owners

## Core Features

1.  Hero with cinematic video
2.  Premium Services
3.  Car Modifications
4.  Accessories
5.  Before & After Gallery
6.  Brands We Service
7.  Testimonials
8.  Instagram Feed
9.  Book Appointment
10. Contact & Google Maps

## Functional Requirements

-   Responsive design
-   WhatsApp integration
-   Contact form
-   Gallery with filters
-   Smooth scrolling
-   SEO optimization

## Non-Functional Requirements

-   Lighthouse score \>90
-   Mobile-first
-   Fast loading
-   Secure forms
-   Accessibility compliant

## Tech Stack

-   React + Vite
-   Tailwind CSS
-   Framer Motion
-   GSAP
-   Lenis
-   Swiper
